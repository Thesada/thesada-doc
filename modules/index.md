# Modules

These are applications built on the framework, each one a module that adds its sensors and alert rules on top of the shared firmware rather than forking it. They double as worked references for building your own. Select a module below for wiring diagrams, BOM, and config.

---

## OWB Monitor

Outdoor wood boiler monitor. Tracks supply and return temperatures on two loops, pump current draw, and sends alerts when the boiler requires attention.

---

## SHT31 Monitor

Temperature and humidity sensor module based on the Sensirion SHT31. Suitable for indoor and sheltered outdoor use.
